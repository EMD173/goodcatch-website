The moment we are in

This time, we are here at the beginning.

Historically, Black folks have found ourselves on the outside of burgeoning technology — arriving after the rules were written, paying rent on tools built elsewhere. This moment is different. Artificial intelligence is coded through language now, and language is what we carry. We have the chance to grab this technology at the very moment it is developing itself — to be builders in it, not just users of it.

There is a setup to learn, real things you have to know. Teaching that setup is what I do.

The idea, plainly.

Move the documentation off the teacher's desk and into agentic AI — running on the phone already in their hand.

1
The teacher speaks the moment.Talk-to-text in seconds, in the hallway, with the child still in front of them. No keyboard, no end-of-day backlog.
2
The agent collects and aggregates.AI organizes the data, tracks the pattern, and routes it where it needs to go — school-specific, teacher-specific, student-specific, at lightspeed.
3
The documentation writes itself.The agent drafts the reports and the paperwork the system requires, so the record is more complete, not less.
The point

This lets a teacher be student-centered, not data-centered — while the data we collect becomes more authentic, more usable, and more true. Better records and more time with children. We no longer have to choose.

Where this goes

The curriculum specialist becomes the AI specialist.

As this technology enters the classroom, the most valuable person in the building is someone who holds both: deep curriculum expertise and a working understanding of how to build and direct agentic AI. An AI specialist sitting alongside teachers would be transformative.

That is the role I am built for — and the work I am already doing.

Built in the immediacy of the need.

Not bought off a shelf from a vendor — built inside the building, on the school’s own data, its own roster, its own intention. Teaching comes out from behind the desktop and into the hand, where the students are. A few needs your buildings already know:

1
The pedagogical gap.We keep bringing people into classrooms without teacher preparation, believing anyone can teach. Most people can learn to — if they can reach the strategies research has shown to work. Picture an agentic coach a newer teacher can ask questions of all day, aligned to the district’s own preparation and intention, so they always know where the work is headed.
2
Differentiation, on demand.A specialist sees teachers struggling to differentiate for multilingual learners — and stands up a tool for that exact hallway, that exact week. Local. Immediate.
3
The paperwork that runs a school.Agentic AI gathers, organizes, and prints what the district requires — the binders, the forms, the Friday recognition list read over the loudspeaker — from moments the adults already captured in three taps.
