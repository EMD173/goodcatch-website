The technology story

Education has too much to do.

So GoodCatch passes much of the do to the intelligence of AI — and gives the human part back to humans.

CaptureRecognition in 3 taps or by voice — so easy it actually happens mid-class. Recognition is the path of least resistance, by design.
PaperworkThe district’s required documentation writes itself from the teacher’s entry. GoodCatch removes work schools are already required to do.
CoachingResearch-based strategies matched to what a behavior is reaching for — the app informs; a human always decides.
AgencyGoodCatch reads the school’s own data and speaks up unprompted:
GoodCatch noticed

“Re-teach moments in the cafeteria doubled this month — want to schedule a Cafeteria Reset week? I found a date that avoids testing windows.”

PBIS — the U.S. Department of Education–backed behavior framework — runs in 25,000+ American schools. It’s required in Birmingham City Schools and promoted statewide by ALSDE. Schools already run the framework. GoodCatch is the missing software layer that makes daily execution possible — lightening the load, lessening stress, widening what teachers have room to teach.

Teachers will need training on it — and that is a door, not a barrier: professional development, adoption, relationships.
